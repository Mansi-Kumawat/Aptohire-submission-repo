02 / AI designed to power AptoHire

AptoHire's product vision uses AI to make skills evidence more useful and talent discovery more relevant. These are planned capabilities unless verified as implemented in the live prototype.

Evidence layer

Project & skill evidence review

Assist with reviewing project descriptions and submitted evidence; flag missing or inconsistent information for human review.

Planned / verify implementation
Talent discovery

Skill-based blind matching

Compare role requirements with relevant skill evidence and help surface candidates while minimizing unnecessary identity exposure early in discovery.

Planned / verify implementation
Learner experience

AI profile assistance

Help learners structure project descriptions, organize skills and identify missing information—without inventing experience.

Planned / verify implementation
Future roadmap

AI judge / skill interview

Future structured role-relevant questions and rubric-based feedback, with human review of assessment signals.

Future capability
How blind matching is intended to work
  1. Organization defines role requirements.
  2. AI compares requirements with permitted skill evidence.
  3. Discovery prioritizes skill relevance and hides unnecessary identity information where feasible.
  4. Authorized recruiter reviews the match and makes the decision.

Fairness guardrail: blind matching does not guarantee bias-free outcomes. Criteria, data quality, privacy, explainability and human review must be tested.

06 / Responsible AI principles

Evidence, not fabrication

AI profile assistance must not invent projects, skills or experience.

Human oversight

Recommendations and future interview feedback support—not replace—human judgment.

Privacy by design

Share only necessary information, respect student permissions and protect evidence.

Fairness and transparency

Test criteria and outcomes; blind matching alone does not remove all bias.

Submission-ready statement

AptoHire used AI-assisted workflows for ideation, planning, market and product analysis, technical documentation, prompt engineering, UI/UX iteration and full-stack prototype development. Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT and Arena AI supported different parts of the workflow, while the team retained responsibility for product decisions, review, manual testing and iteration. Based on the workflow and supporting artifacts, the team estimates approximately 85% of execution activities were AI-assisted; this is a qualitative estimate, not an audited measurement. Within the product vision, AI is intended to support project/skill evidence review, skill-based blind matching and profile assistance, with structured AI-led skill interviews on the future roadmap. Product features will be described as live only after implementation and validation. AI-assisted pitch-deck and demo-video work will be documented after completion.
