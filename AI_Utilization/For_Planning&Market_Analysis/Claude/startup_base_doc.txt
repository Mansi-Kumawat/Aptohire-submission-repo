Reply summary:
I built the blueprint as five Word files, about 65 pages in total.
- 01 Foundations: PDF analysis, contradictions, competitors, positioning, 25
  names, personas, permissions, journeys.
- 02 PRD/MVP/Business: requirement IDs, permission matrix, scope, monetization,
  metrics, roadmap, risks.
- 03 Data and Identity: about 50 tables, six Mermaid diagrams, lifecycle
  scenarios, retention policy.
- 04 Architecture, AI, Security: stack, system and AI design, threat model,
  consent, DPDP status, blind recruitment, analytics.
- 05 Engineering Blueprint: APIs, schemas, repo layout, jobs, tests, backlog,
  decision log.

Main findings about the PDF:
- Market sizing: arithmetic is correct but about 96% of the INR 50.15 Cr comes
  from 800 paying employers, which does not fit a 3-5 employer pilot. The 6:1
  LTV:CAC ratio does not follow from the stated ranges (they imply 60:1+).
- Video-first scoring: AI cannot reliably judge skill or authorship from video;
  design switched to evidence-first (tests, artifacts, optional explanation).
- Student premium (INR 199-499) conflicts with "students should not pay to be
  discoverable".
- "Zero-knowledge" matching adds complexity without MVP benefit.
- Name: keep UniCred only as a codename (close to CRED and UniCredit);
  provisional pick Provena, alternatives Verso and Cairn.
- Regulation: DPDP Rules 2025 notified Nov 2025; core obligations start
  13 May 2027 (checked by search, not against the Gazette).

Limits: names unscreened; competitors only partly verified; legal sections are
guidance, not legal advice; thresholds are starting assumptions.

Questions asked: IT/CS only or also Management; 18+ pilot assumption; who owns
legal review; which model provider.
