# AptoHire — Financial & Operations (Topic 5)

Files:
1. `01_Financial_Model_Projections/01_AptoHire_5_Year_Financial_Model.xlsx`
   - Editable assumptions, 5-year P&L scenario, simplified cash-flow/runway and monthly burn tracker.
2. `02_Unit_Economics/02_AptoHire_Unit_Economics_CAC_LTV.xlsx`
   - University-focused CAC/LTV, gross margin, churn, payback and pilot conversion assumptions.
3. `03_Standard_Operating_Procedures/03_AptoHire_Standard_Operating_Procedures.docx`
   - Eight proposed SOPs: operating rhythm, discovery, releases, privacy/security, onboarding, support/incidents, finance, and AI governance.

Important:
- Financial values are illustrative assumptions, not actual AptoHire data, commitments or validated forecasts.
- Update assumptions with verified quotes, pipeline, customer interviews, contracts and actual costs before using externally.
- The cash flow is intentionally simplified and does not replace an accountant-reviewed cash-flow statement.
- LTV based on 1/churn is a directional approximation and should not be treated as reliable until retention cohorts exist.
- SOPs are proposed working procedures and need named owners, founder approval and legal/security review before formal adoption.
