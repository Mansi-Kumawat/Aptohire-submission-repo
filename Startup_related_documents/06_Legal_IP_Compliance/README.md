Critical guardrails:
- These are working templates, not legal advice, final contracts, a patent application, or certification evidence.
- Complete placeholders and obtain qualified Indian legal review before signing or publishing.
- No specific patentable invention or filing has been confirmed.
- Do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR or DPDP compliance unless scope and evidence support the claim.
- DPDP Act/Rules use phased commencement; confirm current provision-by-provision dates with counsel and the official notifications.
- Privacy policy must match actual data flows, vendors, retention and implemented user controls.
