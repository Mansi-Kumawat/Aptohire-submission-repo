## Research integrity
This package combines public-source context with clearly labeled strategic hypotheses. TAM/SAM/SOM calculations are illustrative bottom-up scenarios, not verified market forecasts. Personas are unvalidated hypotheses because no survey response export or interview transcripts were supplied. Competitor features/pricing can change; verify current claims on official product pages before external publication.
