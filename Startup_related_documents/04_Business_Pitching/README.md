## Important evidence and assumption controls
- University-first customer strategy; students free during early validation; paid pilots may convert to annual institutional licences; onboarding / integration services may be charged; employer subscriptions are a later hypothesis.
- No customer count, revenue, pricing, funding ask, valuation, founder names, ownership percentages, ESOP allocation, or signed contracts were supplied. These are not fabricated.
- Pitch deck clearly distinguishes market context from AptoHire traction and labels product workflows as intended, not necessarily production-ready.
- Market context references: AISHE reports at https://aishe.gov.in/document-category/aishe-final-reports/ and Mercer India Graduate Skill Index 2025 at https://www.mercer.com/insights/talent-and-transformation/talent-assessment/indias-graduate-skill-index-2025/.
- Before investor circulation, complete founder/team details, current product status, verified validation metrics, a funding ask and use-of-funds milestones. Obtain legal/accounting advice before issuing equity or using the cap table for a transaction.
